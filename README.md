## ✨ Why Moslem?

Moslem is built with one goal: **make worship easier, not more complicated.**
No account, no ads, no clutter. Open the app — prayer times, the Quran, and the qibla
direction are right at your fingertips. Available in **Indonesian and English**.

## 🕌 Features

### ⏰ Automatic Prayer Times
- **Automatic location detection** — allow location access once and your schedule adapts to your city. Move to another city? The schedule follows.
- **Countdown to the next adhan**, so you never miss a prayer.
- Highlights the upcoming prayer, complete with Gregorian and Hijri dates.

### 🌅 Illustrations That Follow the Day
The mosque illustration **changes with the prayer times** — dark at dawn, bright at noon,
golden at dusk, and dark again into the night. Like watching the sky outside your window.

### 📖 Complete Quran
- **114 surahs** with Uthmani Arabic script, translation, and transliteration.
- **Per-verse tafsir (exegesis)** — just tap and the explanation appears.
- **Mushaf mode** — turn off translation and read the Quran exactly like a printed mushaf:
  continuous right-to-left text with elegant golden verse markers.
- **Continue reading** — the app remembers your last verse and takes you right back.
- **Per-surah recitation** (Sheikh Mishary Alafasy) to listen while you read.
- Adjustable Arabic font family and size for your reading comfort.

### 🧭 Precise Qibla Compass
- Uses your phone's **full magnetometer** (Absolute Orientation Sensor).
- A golden needle points to the qibla, with the distance to the Kaaba.
- A "✓ facing the qibla" indicator appears when you're aligned.

### 🔔 Adhan Notifications
- Get notified **when a prayer time begins** — even when the app is closed.
- Uses your device's standard notifications: simple and non-intrusive.

### 📲 Installs Like a Native App
Moslem is a **PWA (Progressive Web App)** — open it in your browser, choose *Add to Home
Screen*, and it installs like a native app. Light, fast, and easy on your storage.

### 🌙 Dark & Light Mode
Follows your system theme automatically, or switch with a single tap.

## 🚀 Get Started in 30 Seconds

1. Open **Moslem** in your phone's browser.
2. Allow **location access** — your prayer schedule is instantly accurate.
3. Tap the **⋮ / Share** menu and choose **Add to Home Screen** / **Install**.
4. Tap anywhere on the screen to enable **adhan notifications**.

Done. Moslem is ready to help you pray on time and read the Quran every day.

---

## 🕋 Privacy

Moslem asks for no account, tracks nothing, and stores no personal data.
Your location is only used to compute prayer times and the qibla direction — on your own device.

> **Note:** the Quran translation & tafsir content is currently provided in Indonesian.
> The full app interface is available in English and Indonesian.
